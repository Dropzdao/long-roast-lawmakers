Entry for the make.fun bounty **The Long Roast: Lawmakers and the File They Cannot Convert**
($2, top 10 split, free to enter).

## The entry

> Congress can draft 400 pages of law telling your AI what it may not do, but ask them to turn the bill into a PDF and the most powerful committee in Washington is one intern away from a paper jam.

One sentence, 195 characters — a roast with a real turn: the setup is the
lawmakers' authority (400 pages of AI law), the undercut is the file they
cannot convert, and the punch lands on the last few words.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the line above to `/output/entry.txt`. Nothing else:
deterministic, no network, no input, no dependencies beyond Python 3.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
